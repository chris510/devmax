# Week 1 — start here

Week 1 is ready to run now. It is **20 scheduled hours**. The separate Stretch
menu is optional and does not affect plan progress.

The order matters: study the source, finish its observable work, then use
Unprompted for delayed recall. Do not score a card immediately after seeing its
answer.

## Monday — delivery framework · 3h

1. Open [Hello Interview — Delivery Framework](https://www.hellointerview.com/learn/system-design/in-a-hurry/delivery).
2. Finish the lesson.
3. Close it and write the sequence from memory.
4. Speak one ten-minute requirements-to-deep-dives walkthrough without notes.

This source unlocks the delivery-sequence, non-functional-requirements, and
decision-driven-estimation recall topics.

## Tuesday — API and network boundaries · 3h

### Behavioral evidence inventory · 1h

Create one row for each of these signals: scope, ownership, ambiguity,

real event or mark it missing. Do not write polished scripts yet.

## Friday — Bitly blind-first design · 4h

Do not read the breakdown before the blind attempt.

1. Scope requirements and constraints · 15m.
2. Design blind · 45m.
3. Open [Hello Interview — Design Bitly](https://www.hellointerview.com/learn/system-design/problem-breakdowns/bitly) and compare · 90m.
4. Redraw the complete design · 60m.
5. Self-grade delivery, mechanisms, trade-offs, and failures; record three
   concrete differences · 30m.

## Saturday — repair and retrieve · 3h

### Sourced repair · 2h

Debrief the Bitly and Python work. Choose the highest-impact observed gap,
repair it from a cited source, and propose no more than three focused recall
cards. A broad design prompt is not a card.

### Closed-book retrieval · 1h

Without notes:

- reconstruct the delivery sequence;
- explain one API trust boundary;
- trace one ambiguous network write;
- distinguish two pointers, sliding window, and intervals from fresh prompt
  descriptions;
- record every miss.

## How Unprompted fits

For each source lesson:

1. Open Today → Plan → Week 1 → the lesson.
2. Open the listed source, do the `DONE WHEN` work, and return to the app.
3. Tap `Mark complete`. Opening the source alone never marks progress.
4. The lesson's mapped Unprompted concepts become tappable. Open each one; when
   it says `Learn first`, use the card-owned Learn action.
5. Stop there. The first scored review waits until the later of eight hours
   after exposure and the next local day, then appears through normal Today
   review. Subsequent reviews follow the normal schedule.

The six Week 1 mappings already resolve to approved cards. A future mapping that
says `Not ready` has no action: it still needs reviewed answer authority and
deliberate activation, and completing a Plan item never bypasses that gate.

Coding implementations and full designs remain external evidence. They produce
zero to three focused gap cards only when practice reveals a mechanism that
cannot be reconstructed and trusted answer authority exists.

## Stretch, only if wanted

The optional menu is up to 20 additional hours: eight hours of unseen Python
problems from the same three families, six hours of additional blind designs,
three hours on a current-week source or case study, two hours rehearsing one
evidence-backed behavioral story, and one hour harvesting gaps. Stretch should
deepen Week 1, not begin Week 2 early.
