# Behavioral story catalog

Complete this outside Devmax before creating behavioral cards. One experience
may support several signal areas, but the catalog should contain enough distinct
material that every answer does not collapse into the same project.

## Story template

### Working title

One line that makes the story easy to retrieve.

### Primary signal

Choose one: scope, ownership, ambiguity, perseverance, conflict resolution,
growth, communication, leadership.

### Context

- What was the business or user problem?
- What made it difficult?
- What was the scale relative to your level?
- What was your responsibility?

### Actions

- What did **you** decide or do?
- Which alternatives did you reject?
- Whom did you need to influence?
- What technical or organizational constraint changed your approach?

### Results

- What measurable outcome changed?
- What did the team or business gain?
- What evidence shows that your actions caused the result?

### Learnings

- What would you do differently?
- How did this change your later behavior?
- Which weakness or mistaken assumption did you discover?

### Follow-up pressure test

- What was the hardest trade-off?
- Who disagreed and why?
- What did you personally own versus the team?
- What failed?
- What would the other person say?
- How does this demonstrate senior scope?

## Generic transfer prompts

Create these only after the catalog exists:

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Describe a project you are most proud of.
3. Describe a conflict and how you handled it.

The story-specific cards establish reliable evidence. These three prompts test
selection and delivery when the question is broad.
